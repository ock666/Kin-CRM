How it works

  1. You upload your Instagram JSON zip. Kin finds your 1:1 conversations (group chats are counted but skipped for now).
  2. You say who each person is — link them to someone already in Kin, or create a new person. Only linking ever updates your data, and only the date you last talked.
  3. You can ask for suggestions. Kin reads a short slice of the conversation and proposes profile details (job, interests, people in their life…). Each suggestion is shown with the reason and saved only when you tap Accept.
