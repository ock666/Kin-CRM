🧘 Regulation toolkit

Quick grounding when things feel overwhelming. Use anytime — no one's keeping score.

🌱 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

Engage senses to anchor in the moment.

  1. 5 things you can see
  2. 4 things you can feel
  3. 3 things you can hear
  4. 2 things you can smell
  5. 1 thing you can taste

Take your time. No rush. Try to really notice each one.

🫁 Box breathing

Inhale → hold → exhale → hold (4sec each).

🧠 Facts vs. the RSD story

When your anxiety is telling you a story — try this:

Facts (observable):

  • What actually happened? No interpretation.
  • What did they say or do, word for word?
  • What's the evidence?

Story (anxiety's version):

  • What meaning am I attaching to it?
  • What am I assuming they think?
  • What would I tell a friend in my shoes?

Notice the gap between them — they're often different things.

🧊 Physical grounding

Quick resets you can do anywhere:

  • Run cold water over your wrists for 30 seconds
  • Press your feet firmly into the floor — feel the ground
  • Squeeze and release your fists 5 times
  • Notice the temperature of the air on your skin
  • Stretch your neck gently side to side

🕊️ Reminders

Feeling overwhelmed is not a moral failure. Taking space is allowed. You don't have to fix it right now.

If you need someone to talk to right now, crisis lines are available — 988 (US) or 111 (NHS UK).
