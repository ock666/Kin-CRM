🛡️ Privacy & your data

Kin is designed to keep your relationships — and the details about them — close. Here's where everything lives and how it stays yours.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored in Kin's own database on the server you run it on. There is no third-party cloud where your people, notes, or journal are shared by default.

What's stored

  • People profiles, tags, notable dates, and notes
  • Journal entries, photos (as references), and check-in history
  • Gamification progress (level, XP, achievements)
  • Optional push-notification subscriptions on devices you enable

You can see and delete any of it, and export everything at any time.

Self-hosting

Kin runs as a single container (FastAPI + SQLite/Postgres). It's made to be self-hosted: run it on your own server, keep the data directory on your own storage, reach it over HTTPS (e.g. via a Cloudflare Tunnel so install & push work). Nothing phones home.

Optional third-party connections

Only if you turn them on in Settings:

  • AI assistant — sends journal/context snippets to the AI endpoint you configure (your API key stays on your server).
  • Immich — pulls in your own photo memories from your own Immich server.

Your rights & backups

You can export everything as JSON or CSV any time, and import it back. Deleting a person removes their record; there's also a gentle "stepping back" mode if you need a break from reminders.
